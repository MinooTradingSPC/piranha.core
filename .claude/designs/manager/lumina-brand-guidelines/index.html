Primary

Our primary logomark stands as the definitive symbol of our corporate identity. As our most identifiable asset, it must anchor all primary communications. This mark distills our core essence — encompassing our ethos, character, and future trajectory.

For strict brand cohesion, the logo must invariably be presented in its native configuration, scale, and colorway. Under no circumstances should the mark be modified, warped, or manually redrawn.
